What can be deleted, and in what order. Changes preview instantly; Save applies them right away.

What cleanup may touch. Movies score per title, TV per season, on one shared scale.

Movies join the one deletion order, scored per title; the lowest-scoring are marked, then deleted once the deletion delay ends. Off = movies are never deleted.
Seasons join the same order, scored per season; the lowest-scoring are marked, then deleted once the deletion delay ends. Off = seasons are never deleted.

Lowest retention score deletes first — movies and seasons alike.
